ShieldBlock Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

The short version: ShieldBlock Pro does everything on your device. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data — ever. We never see which sites you visit or what gets blocked.

1. Who we are

ShieldBlock Pro ("the Extension", "we") is a free, independent browser extension developed by Bronson Bissell. It blocks ads, trackers, and cookie-consent banners across the web and on streaming platforms (YouTube, Twitch, Spotify, Hulu, Kick, and Amazon). Questions? Contact zombiekillacrew@gmail.com.

2. The data the Extension stores (all on your device)

The Extension keeps the following only in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local and the browser's built-in IndexedDB). None of it is ever sent to us or anyone else:

  • Settings & whitelist — which features are on, sites you've paused or allowed, and toggle states.
  • Statistics — counts of items blocked per platform and an estimate of time saved. These are aggregate numbers, not a record of the pages you visited.
  • Diagnostic log — a short rolling log of extension activity (kept locally in IndexedDB) that powers the in-popup "health check." It never leaves your device.
  • Filter-list cache — downloaded ad-filter lists and the blocking rules and cosmetic selectors parsed from them, cached so the Extension works fast and offline.
  • Your custom rules — any filters you type or elements you hide with the picker.

3. Optional settings sync

If you choose to enable it, the Extension can sync only your settings and whitelist across your own browsers using the browser's built-in account sync (chrome.storage.sync). This is handled entirely by your browser vendor (Google or Mozilla) under their privacy policy — it does not pass through any server we operate, and it never includes browsing history, statistics, or logs.

4. What we never collect

  • Your browsing history or the URLs you visit
  • Page content, form data, keystrokes, or credentials
  • Any personally identifiable information
  • A record of which ads were blocked on which sites
  • Crash reports, telemetry, or analytics of any kind

5. Network requests the Extension makes

The Extension connects to the internet only to download public, non-personalized data:

  • Filter lists — it periodically fetches ad-blocking lists from public repositories (EasyList, uBlock Origin, AdGuard, and regional lists). These requests carry no identifiers and the results are cached locally.
  • Safe Browsing lists — it downloads lists of known malware/phishing domains and checks sites locally against that downloaded list. The URLs you visit are not sent anywhere for this check.

The Extension does not connect to any server operated by us, any analytics service, or any data broker.

6. No remote code

The Extension never downloads or executes remote code. Filter lists are treated strictly as data. Ad-defusing "scriptlets" are a fixed set of named functions shipped inside the Extension and called by name — there is no eval() and no remotely loaded script.

7. Permissions, and why each is needed

declarativeNetRequest — block ad/tracker requests and strip tracking URL parameters, without reading page content.
declarativeNetRequestFeedback — count blocked requests so the popup and badge can show a number. No URLs leave your device.
scripting — inject cosmetic ad-hiding CSS and platform ad-removal scripts, and run the "hide element" picker you trigger.
storage / unlimitedStorage — save settings and the large local filter/cosmetic caches (which can exceed the default storage quota) so blocking stays reliable.
tabs / webNavigation — apply per-site rules at the right time and update the per-tab block badge. Used for tab id/URL only; nothing is logged or sent.
alarms — schedule periodic filter-list and Safe Browsing refreshes and expire temporary pauses.
contextMenus — provide the right-click "hide element" and "block on this site" actions.
host access (<all_urls>) — ads appear on every site, so rules must be able to apply anywhere. The Extension only blocks/hides ad elements; it does not read or transmit page content.

8. Third parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data, because we never collect any. The only external parties involved are the maintainers of the public filter lists the Extension downloads (EasyList, uBlock Origin, AdGuard, and regional list authors); those downloads contain no information that could identify you.

9. Children's privacy

The Extension collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

10. Your rights & controls

Because all data lives on your device and we hold nothing, you are always in control:

  • Reset statistics or clear logs anytime from the popup's Support tab.
  • Export your settings, stats, or a diagnostic snapshot from the popup whenever you like.
  • Uninstalling the Extension permanently deletes all of its local storage and IndexedDB data.

Under the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA, requests for access, deletion, or portability are satisfied entirely by these on-device controls — there is no server-side copy of your data for us to produce or erase.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Because we collect no personal data, changes are expected to be infrequent.

12. Contact
