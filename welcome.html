Two features are off by default — enable them in Settings
  • Paywall Bypass — Removes CSS-overlay soft paywalls on news sites. Disabled by default since users may have paid subscriptions.
  • Timezone Spoofing — Reports UTC to all sites to prevent timezone-based fingerprinting. Disabled by default as it breaks calendar apps like Google Calendar.
Quick tips
Open ShieldBlock popup Alt + Shift + S
Pause blocking on this site Alt + Shift + B
Pick an element to hide Alt + Shift + P

Found an ad that slipped through? Right-click it and choose ShieldBlock: Hide this element — your selection syncs across sessions.

Zero data collection. ShieldBlock Pro processes everything locally on your device. No browsing history, no personal data, and no telemetry is ever sent to any server. Filter lists are fetched directly from their public sources.
