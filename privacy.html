ShieldBlock Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated June 1, 2026

In one sentence: ShieldBlock Pro runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no tracking — so there is simply nothing for us to collect, see, or sell.

1 What stays on your device

Everything the extension remembers is saved locally in your browser and never leaves your computer:

  • Your settings — which features are on and your list of trusted sites.
  • Block statistics — how many ads were blocked and an estimate of time saved.
  • A diagnostic log — a rolling 7-day activity log shown in the popup, for troubleshooting.
  • Filter list cache — public ad-blocking lists, saved so pages load fast without re-downloading.

2 What we store vs. what we never touch

✓ Stored locally

  • Settings & trusted sites
  • Block counts & time saved
  • 7-day diagnostic log
  • Cached filter lists

✕ Never collected

  • Your browsing history or URLs
  • Page content, forms, or keystrokes
  • Any personal or identifying data
  • Telemetry, analytics, or crash reports

3 The only network connections

The extension reaches the internet for one reason only: to periodically download public ad-filter lists (such as EasyList, uBlock Origin, and AdGuard). These downloads carry no information about you and are cached on your device. There is no ShieldBlock server to connect to — because one doesn't exist.

4 Permissions, in plain English

  • Block ads & trackers — filters network requests without reading page content.
  • Save settings — keeps your preferences and stats on your device.
  • Detect page changes — so rules apply correctly and the badge count updates.
  • Run on streaming sites — removes ads on YouTube, Twitch, Spotify, Hulu, and Kick.
  • Scheduled refresh — keeps filter lists up to date in the background.
  • Right-click element picker — lets you hide anything you point at.
  • Access to all sites — required so blocking can work everywhere you browse.

5 You're always in control

You can erase everything at any time. Open the popup and use Settings → Reset Stats / Clear Logs, or simply uninstall the extension — that removes all locally stored data automatically. No request, no waiting, no copy kept anywhere.

6 Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the date above is updated. Because we collect no personal data, meaningful changes should be rare. Questions? Reach the developer, Bronson Bissell, through the support link in the extension's store listing.
